    About Dice Tower

    We started Dice Tower for the students and card players who never had a comfortable place to play. Good coffee, big tables, and a shelf of games you can borrow for the afternoon.

    New to tabletop games? Ask at the counter and we will teach you a game in ten minutes. Already have a deck? Bring it. Singles, sleeves and playmats are on the shelf by the door.

    • Magic: The Gathering and other TCGs, played here every week
    • Board game library free to play with any order
    • Student friendly prices and long, quiet afternoons
