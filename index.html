The menu

Every drink is priced like a mana cost. Cheap and easy to cast, so you can keep your wallet for booster packs.

Good beans, long sessions

We started Dice Tower for the people who used to take over a corner of the nearest milk tea shop for six hours. Players, students, and dungeon masters deserve a place that is happy to see them stay.

Our coffee leans on Batangas barako and single-origin beans from Philippine highland farms, roasted in small batches and pulled by baristas who also know what a mulligan is.

Inside, it feels like your favorite game store: shelves of board games to borrow, playmat-sized tables, warm lamps, and a wall of binders for trading.
